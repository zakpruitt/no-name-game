## How to play it

- `godot --path .` boots into training (four dummies that never die). Esc: MOVES (every string, plus an
  IMPROVISING section), Fill Awakening, Settings, Restart training.
- Camera: `godot --path . -- --cam-dist=15 --cam-fov=38 --cam-frame=1.3`. `--cam-dist` metres (13 default),
  `--cam-fov` degrees (37), `--cam-frame` how far the fight framing may pull out (1.25; 1 turns it off).
- `--spar=rogue` (or `minion`, `warrior`, `shield`) adds one live foe at 1/3 damage that respawns.
- `--hero=heretic` plays round 28's full-size heretic instead of the chibi.
- `--bag-damping=N` (and `--bag-stiffness=`, `--bag-lean=`, `--bag-squash=`) tunes the dummies' wobble.
- `--start-awakened` starts with a full meter. Flags combine: `godot --path . -- --spar=rogue --cam-dist=14`.
