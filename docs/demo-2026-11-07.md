# Demo path, 2026-11-07

A 10-15 minute hand-played tour of the current build, escalating from single swings to the room combos. Every
step names the launch preset, where to stand, the exact presses, what you should see and the clip in
`Desktop\awaken-combo-clips\` that shows it. The reel `00 Demo reel 2026-11-07.mp4` follows the same order.

## Launching

From the integration checkout (`C:\Users\pruit\Desktop\dev\awaken`), in a terminal:

```
tools\demo.bat 1        plain room, full kit
tools\demo.bat 2        room with pieces, full kit, a live warrior (spar)
tools\demo.bat 3        flooded cistern, full kit
tools\demo.bat 4        forge hall, full kit
tools\demo.bat 5        room with pieces, full kit, a live archer
tools\demo.bat 6        plain room, full kit, a live warrior
tools\demo.bat N bot    the autotest bot plays every string in preset N (1-2 min, quits by itself)
tools\demo.bat 3 show   the bot plays the cistern's showcase (also 4: the forge's two)
```

Every preset is `--kit=full` (the demoted upgrades and matrix cells on). Without the script: `godot --path .
-- --kit=full --room=<plain|pieces|cistern|forge> [--spar=warrior|archer]`; or Esc > Settings > "Room (on restart)"
then Restart training (that choice is saved; the flag is for one run only).

## Controls cheat sheet

| Input | Does |
|---|---|
| WASD | move (he aims at the mouse when you swing) |
| LMB (L) | light: axe (storm), spear (ice), hammer (magma) as L, L L, L L L |
| RMB (H) | heavy: the tentacle (dark). Hold RMB = Overwhelm |
| Space (D) | dash; into a foe's strike = the counter |
| R | swing round a pillar (hold to go further, let go to fling) |
| Q | Awaken (meter full; Esc > Fill Awakening fills it) |
| T | slow motion: full, 50%, 25% (good for showing a breaker) |
| Esc | pause: MOVES panel (every input), Fill Awakening, Settings, Restart training, Quit |

Reading the notation: `L L L H H` is LMB LMB LMB RMB RMB in rhythm. "In the window" means: after a big move he
leaps and a circle with key chips appears (`[LMB] X or [RMB] Y`); press the button as the ring closes, PERFECT on
the beat. "Pause" means stop pressing for a moment (the move's own window opens). The mouse cursor picks the
target for the bracketed endings (Pinwheel's wall or pillar, Meteor Fall's pile, Reap's hook).

## If something looks off

- **No window circle after a big move:** the string dropped (too slow, or a press too early). The combo guide shows
  the next press; Esc > MOVES lists every input.
- **L L vaults instead of swinging:** a Frost Spear still stands (5 s). Wait it out, or use it (L L H L vaults on
  purpose).
- **The other ending fired:** RMB is the first chip, LMB the second; the names are under the circle.
- **Pinwheel / Slapshot fly into open floor:** nothing in reach (10 m). Face a wall or pillar; check the bracket.
- **The cistern shot stops short:** the cubes were not on the channel or he did not face along it. Use `demo 3 show`.
- **The warrior isn't attacking:** it is held (a carry, bind, cube or hook stops its strikes) or respawning (0.8 s).
  It hits at 1/3 damage.
- **The counter does nothing:** too early (the ring must be nearly full) or not aimed at it (within 60 deg). Trying
  costs nothing.
- **Everything is slow:** T cycles slow motion; press it until full speed is back.
- **Wrong room:** demo.bat always passes `--room=`; the Settings choice only applies without the flag.
- **Input dead:** click into the window once (it may have lost focus).
- **"YOU FELL":** the spar foe got him (the archer chips away if you stand still); the room restarts by itself in
  2 s.
- **Anything stuck:** Esc > Restart training, or close and rerun the preset.
