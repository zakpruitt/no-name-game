# Round 3, 2026-11-06: what landed and what needs you

Your ask before leaving: "improving the heretics combos and really focus on a refinement of the art style and
effects. they look weird to me. air needs to be clearly air but stylistic and make lightning combos more a part of
everything. i specifically want one where the tentacle conducts lightning and zaps everybody ... take a look at the
heretic model it sucks and is super buggy in game." Seven lanes ran (all on Opus, planned and reviewed from the
orchestrating session). Everything is on `dev`, local only, nothing pushed. All of it was judged from headless

Why it looked weird (what the first film showed): wind and lightning shared one green; floor hoops read as debug
rings; the tentacles were pipes; the hero's face was a decal that floated off the head; drawn and polygon effects
were mixed in the same string.

## What landed, per ask

- **The tentacle conducts lightning and zaps everybody: Galvanize** (`H L L`, move `XC`). The tentacles latch every
  foe within 8 m (11 m if Reap-marked, six links at most), charge for a beat, then all discharge at once. Damage 7
  per foe, +30% per other link. Word: BZZRAKK! Clips `20 Lightning ...`.
- **Lightning in everything**: Live Wire's pulses now run down the tentacle curves (`scripts/live_current.gd`). A
  new `charged` state (3 s) pays with every other element: Overload (storm), Superconduct (ice), Arc Flash (magma,
  throws the neighbours), Conduct (dark). Short Circuit is a second (LMB) ending on Live Wire's and Grounded's
  breaker window. Bot strings 24-29 play them; autotest `strings=30/30 breakers=11/11`.
- **Air is clearly air** (`scripts/designed_twister.gd`, `fx_palette.AIR`): pale white-mint ribbons, swirls, wisps
  and debris, with depth-sorted ribbons round the foe. Lightning moved to electric lime with a white-hot core, and
  any bolt falling more than 3 m is a sky strike (leader, held bolt, afterimage, scorch). Clips `19 Air a` to `l`.
- **Art style and effects put together**: the tentacle is a smooth tapered limb with a smear; ground stamps are
  broken brush arcs; the breaker cue is a thin paper target; the rank-up, hop-landing and crater hoops are gone.
  Ice and magma are drawn sheets (Molten Palm fist and lava seam, geysers, meteor, ice blocks and spikes). The
  Awakening is a drawn crown, gather lines and floor sunburst; chain halos, daze stars, the boulder, the steam cloud
  and the Eruption / Anvil landings are drawn. Clips `21 Cohesion`, `22 Elements`, `23 Finish`.
- **The hero model** (`tools/hero_build/hero_v2.py`): rebuilt as a horned chibi cultist on the same rig and clips.
  The face is modelled, not a decal, so nothing floats. Axe at 0.8 size, hood squash damped. Clips `18 Hero model v2`.
- **Combat feel** (`docs/history/feel-audit-2026-11-06.md`, clips `24 Feel ...`): Live Wire's hang no longer stutters, the
  combo guide stays off the pile (an old bug), ender names move to the top band in a hang, the lava well ticks
  quietly, Short Circuit's far target is in frame, Maw's damage total stays on the page.
