Unique spins

The spirit is the one part of the run build that is Awaken's own. Boons and Gear are typical. Four lanes each took one angle on making the rest as much ours as the spirit is. This page lists what they proposed, where they agree and clash, and one package to react to. It is written from the lanes' reports and page outlines; the pages have the detail.

Give each layer a different place to live. Moves live in the spirit. Boons live on the spirit: it eats them, and its rank is how much it has eaten. One Gear slot stays a plain find on the arm. The other stops changing him and changes the rooms ahead.

Let the fight steer the offer a little. How the room's last foe dies picks one card's sign. Second breaker endings are learned by landing the first in the right situation, not picked from a card.

The second element is a spirit the arm swallows. That keeps one spirit on his shoulder and still opens the crossovers.

Everything else stays conventional on purpose: 1 of 3, kept all run, plain affix numbers, no rerolls.

The four lanes

The spirit is the build

Boons and gear run through the creature. Six proposals. Its pick: Morsels, then the swallowed spirit, then the second blow once boons are judged.

The heretic's fiction

The church, the prison, the arm and the shackle reshape the systems. Six proposals. Its pick: Keys of the Prison with the Catechism's wording on top.

Earned in the fight

The build comes from how you play, not a card screen. Five proposals, four more considered and cut. Its pick: The Window Learns, with The Last Word beside it.

The world builds back

Rooms, foes and wardens as the build, plus borrows from other genres. Seven proposals. Its pick: Breaches, with a warden's Writ as the floor reward.

Where the lanes agree

The lanes worked apart. Where two or more landed on the same idea, that is the best signal on this page.

  • The rooms are the missing layer. Keys of the Prison, Breaches, Writs and the Augury all move part of the build out of the hero and into the prison. His moves already act on pieces (braziers, grates, kegs, pillars, the gallows), and three flourishes sit switched off. No action roguelike we named does this as a core layer. It also needs real rooms, so it cannot come first.
  • The fight should steer the offer. Lessons (what you did all room) and The Last Word (how the last foe died) are the same idea at two sizes. Both keep the pick and remove some of the dice.
  • Elites carry the gear. Trophy Grafts (the killing move picks the line) and Bearers (he fights with it first) fit together as one drop rule.
  • The church answers the build. Writ of Anathema and Specialists. Both lanes also say not to build it early.
  • Every lane kept the same things plain. One of three, kept all run, fixed affix numbers, the arm's drop-and-compare, no rerolls. Nobody proposed a new currency before slice 9.
  • Every lane hit the same hole: the built spirit holds one element; the drafts assume two per run. See decision 1.

Where they clash

Boon skin: Morsels, worded as the Catechism

Morsels makes a boon a thing the spirit eats. The Catechism makes it a tenet he tears out. Take both: the scrap is a torn page, the card reads tenet then heresy, the spirit eats it. One picture, not two menus.

Catechism alone

Cheaper, text only. Cost: boons stay a card screen beside the spirit, and the spirit's rank needs its own reward.

Room-changers are the wrist slot (Keys)

Keys puts them on the Shackle; Breaches puts them in the Boon offer. On the Shackle they give Gear a job Boons cannot do, which answers the hub's "Gear and Boons may blur" risk. Breaches' list is the item pool.

Room-changers are Boons (Breaches)

No second gear slot to build. Cost: they are kept all run, so you cannot change course, and both gear slots stay conventional.

Spirit rank: meals (Morsels)

Rank II at 2 boons, rank III at 4. One reward stream, and a struggling player ranks up at the same pace.

Spirit rank: deeds (Spirit Vows)

More of a combo game's idea, and it costs nothing to break a vow. Keep it as an early rank-up on top of meals if meals feel flat.

The test for anything added later: does it change a decision in the fight or at the door, or only the word on the card? Morsels alone is mostly words. It earns its place because it settles how the spirit ranks up.

Order

This fits inside The run's ten slices; it does not replace them. The first two steps can be judged in the training room. The rest wait for real rooms.

  1. The boon offer in training, as Morsels

    Three scraps, the spirit eats one, six of the built cells, tenet-then-heresy text, Patch Notes lines. Rank II at 2 meals opens the big move. Judge: does a meal change the next minute, and does the rank feel earned?

  2. One learned ending

    One second ending (the lane lists seven) learned by landing the first in its situation, against dummies or a spar foe. Judge: do you notice the situation, or only the unlock?

  3. Rooms, and The Last Word

    Run slice 1 (three real rooms). The last kill picks one card's sign. Judge: do you start finishing rooms on purpose?

  4. The swallowed spirit

    When floor 2 exists. Judge: do two runs with different pairs feel like two characters?

  5. Gear: the Graft, then Keys

    Run slice 5. Elites carry the Graft. Then two or three Keys from Breaches' list. Judge: does a Key change how you fight the next room?

  6. The first Writ

    Run slice 7, the Turnkey. Judge: is a rule taken from a warden a better floor reward than a boon offer?

Decisions for Zak

  1. Does a run hold two elements, the second as a spirit the arm swallows? Yes. One spirit only would park the three two-element crossovers for good.
  2. The fiction: the church kept each power in a living keeper, and three fled into the prison when he broke in? Yes. It fits "he took the four powers" and "he wins them back from spirits".
  3. Are boons what the spirit eats, and is its rank its meal count? Yes. It replaces a separate rank-up reward.
  4. Do room-changing items go on the wrist slot (Keys) or in the Boon offer (Breaches)? The wrist slot. The Shackle's dash and counter lines move to the Graft.
  5. Are second breaker endings learned in the fight? Try one ending in training before deciding for all seven.
  6. Is step 1 above the next thing to build? Yes, after the bond prompt lands.

Not yet

The second blow. The spirit acting in fights is the strongest "ours" idea left on the table and the dearest: a new behaviour on every breaker. Judge boons first.
The Combo Book and the Haul. Each is a whole new layer. The old run was cut for being wide before the combat was good.
The church answering the build (Writ of Anathema, Specialists). Both lanes that proposed it say to wait. It needs a run that already works.
Confession, the Arm Eats, Temperament, the Augury. Currency, salvage, memory between runs and a map. All four are things the drafts left out on purpose.
Words without play. Three of the "In" rows are mostly presentation (Morsels, the Catechism, Patch Notes). If the package stops there, the build is typical with good names. The parts that change decisions are Keys, the learned endings and the swallowed spirit, and two of those need rooms.

Doc problems the lanes found

  • The hub, kits, loot and run pages predate the spirits. They say "Attunement" and never mention a spirit. Once decision 1 is answered they need one pass.
  • docs/features/spirits.md lists the wrong moves. It gives storm "Tempest" and magma "Cataclysm". The move list has both as the two endings of Fault Line, the storm and magma crossover, and spirits.gd closes a crossover unless both elements are held. Magma's big move, Undertow, is missing from the list. The code reads right; the doc is the suspect. Not checked by running the game.
  • Ice names differ: spirits.md calls Dash L H Ice Cube; element-matrix.md calls it Harpoon and puts Ice Cube one press later.
  • Two things are called "spirit": the creature, and the spectral axe echoes (spirit_echo.gd, spirit-casts.md). The code also calls the spirits wind, water and earth.
  • run.html's foe table leaves out the brute and the cultist.
