The heretic's fiction

One of the unique-spin lanes. It starts from who he is: a novice who robbed the church's vaults, paid the dark with his arm, was chained in the prison built for the powers he stole, and broke the shackle. The question is which of those facts can change a decision the player makes, not only the word on a card.

Make the Shackle slot the prison's keys. Each Shackle piece is something a warden carried to run the prison: a lamplighter's hook, a sluice key, a gaoler's chain. Wearing it changes the rooms ahead, not the hero: braziers start lit, grates are bonded, every wall gets a hook. The Graft keeps Gear and boons' job (how a move works).

This gives Gear a job Boons cannot do, which is the hub's top layer risk ("Gear and Boons may blur"). It uses room data that already exists (ROOM_DEFS layers, links, flourishes, piece states). It is the world doc's own line made into loot: "the hero did not change, the prison did."

For free, present every Boon as a heresy: a line of the church's catechism he tears out. Same pool, same cards, new words.

Later, if the Boon offer feels flat: one grave heresy per offer, stronger, that costs a move from his kit. The dark took his arm as its price. This is the same deal, smaller.

The look rules hold everywhere on this page (docs/characters/heretic.md sections 2 and 8): no ornaments, no keys hanging off him, no relics on his belt. Only the arm and the wrist shackle change, and only in colour and band, as Gear and boons already proposes.

1. The Torn Catechism

Every Boon is a line of the church's doctrine, and taking it means tearing it out.

Reskin Boons

How it works in play

  • The offer is three torn strips from the church's book. Each shows the church's tenet on top, in the church's voice, and his heresy under it. The heresy is the Boon's real rule, in the move list's words.
  • The ten signs become the catechism's ten articles: Of the Frozen, Of the Charged, Of the Molten, Of the Bound, Of the Buried, Of the Raised (airborne), Of the Bell (breakers), Of Answering (the counter), Of the Step (the dash), Of Waking (the Awakening). Icons stay the foe-state marks from comic_state_tell.gd, so the card still reads at a glance.
  • Taking one: the arm snatches the strip and eats it. One short beat on the card screen, nothing in the fight.
  • The pause page ("This run") is the book with his pages missing. A rank II number boon is the same heresy "repeated".

Shard Cut

Of the Frozen. Data-only, shard_cut. C0

Tenet: What the cold takes, it keeps still.

Heresy: The ice cuts. Dark on a frozen foe crushes the ice and the shards cut the foe beside it.

Overload

Of the Charged. Data-only, overload. C0

Tenet: The storm is bled into cold iron.

Heresy: The storm comes back all at once. Any storm hit sets off every charged foe within 8 m.

Riposte Chain

Of Answering. New, C2

Tenet: The penitent does not strike back.

Heresy: He always strikes back. After a counter, your next counter is perfect anywhere in the window for 2 s.

Worked example

  1. Room 1He bonds the ice spirit. No offer yet.
  2. Room 2Of the Frozen, Of the Step, Of the Bell. He tears "The ice cuts" (Shard Cut).
  3. Room 4"Lean in" puts Of the Frozen first again: "The cold walks" (Brittle Crowd).
  4. PauseThe book shows two pages gone from Of the Frozen. That is his build, in one look.
  • Replaces: Boon names and the offer screen's dressing. Nothing in the rules.
  • Reuses: the whole Boon plan, the 18 cells, Kit.set_on(), every word and tell.
  • Cost: C0 per boon (two lines of text). The card screen's skin is a one-time C1 to C2 (paper strip, a tear, the arm's snatch as a UI flourish, not an in-world effect).
  • Risk: text bloat. Tenets must stay at 8 words or fewer, or the card stops being readable in three seconds. Write the heresy line first; drop the tenet if it does not fit.
  • Nearest: Hades' boons, each framed as a god's gift with a voice line; Blasphemous' prayers and rosary beads. Different here: nothing is given. The church never offers; he takes. It changes no decision, and that is fine for a reskin.

2. Keys of the Prison

Shackle gear is what the wardens carried to run the prison, and wearing it changes the rooms ahead, not him.

Departure Gear: the Shackle slot

How it works in play

  • The slot stays. Two slots, three rarities, the floor card, the E swap: all as Gear and boons has them. Only what a Shackle line does changes.
  • A Shackle line is a room key. It uses one of the four kinds the world doc lists (docs/design/environment-lore.md, "How items could alter rooms"): change a piece's state, add a layer, turn a rule on, keep what was used. Each is one key on a room in ROOM_DEFS.
  • It applies from the next room on, while worn. Take it off and the next room is plain again.
  • The door shows it. Run slice 6's door card already shows the reward behind it. With a key worn, it also shows the room's piece icons, with the key's change marked ("+2 grates, bonded").
  • The filter: a key is offered only if its pieces answer to the dark or the spirit he holds (env_piece.EXCLUSIVE). A storm-less run never finds the grate key.
  • The dash and counter lines move to the Graft. They name a move (Backlash, Ricochet, Tow Line, the dash), which is the Graft's test anyway. The Graft pool grows from 8 to 12; the weakest four of the sixteen are cut.

Sample keys

Lamplighter's Hook

Shackle, Iron. Piece state. C1

Every brazier and torch starts lit. Magma has fire to spill at once (brazier_spill), storm has torches to conduct through.

Sapper's Tally

Shackle, Iron. Add a layer. C1

Every room also places the forge hall's powder line: 3 barrels between two braziers. One spark walks the line.

Bonding Iron

Shackle, Hallowed. Layer + rule. C1 + C1

+2 grates in every room, and every room links its grates (the forge's rule): storm on one is storm on all.

Sluice Key

Shackle, Hallowed. Layer + rule. C1 + C1

Every room gets a pool along its back wall, and pools are linked (the cistern's rule): ice on one runs the channel, and a cube struck down it does not stop.

Gaoler's Chain

Shackle, Profane. Rule. Rite: C2

Rite: every room has hooks and the gallows flourish, and hooks hang any foe a breaker throws, not only Ricochet's target. Affix: +1 hook per room.

Turnkey's Ring

Shackle, Profane. Keep what was used. Rite: C3

Rite: a pillar he topples stays down, as a fallen wall in the open, in every later room of the floor and in the warden's room. The prison comes apart behind him and he fights in the wreck.

Worked example

  1. F1 room 1Bonds the storm spirit.
  2. F1 room 2The chest gives Bonding Iron. He swaps it in.
  3. F1 room 3The drill hall has 4 grates, bonded. Updraft Lash (L H) on one makes all of them live. Foes walking in come out charged; one more storm hit is Arc through the room.
  4. DoorCistern or forge. The forge already bonds its plates, so the key adds grates to a room that was storm's anyway. He takes the cistern instead, where the key gives storm floor it never had.
  5. TurnkeyThe warden's room has bonded grates too. The fight is about luring it onto iron.
  • Replaces: Gear and boons' 8 Shackle lines (they move to the Graft pool). The Shackle's look (band metal by rarity) stays.
  • Reuses: rooms.gd ROOM_DEFS (layers, links, flourishes, rebuild), place()'s per-piece state, env_flourish.gd (gallows, ice_rink, brazier_spill), the structure indicator, the chest piece. The world doc already names Sapper's Map, Cold Iron Bond, Water Table, Rusted Chain and Fallen Column; these are those, as Shackle lines.
  • Cost: state and layer keys C1 each (data on a room). Linking rules C1 (they exist for the forge and cistern). Gaoler's Chain C2. Turnkey's Ring C3 (used-up pieces must carry across rooms). Door piece icons: one C2, shared by every key.
  • Risk: it leans on real rooms existing. Gear and boons parked room-piece boons for exactly this reason. A key half fixes it, because a layer key brings its own pieces. Second risk: a room change is felt less than a move change. Test: over ten runs, does Zak ever pick a door because of his key?
  • Nearest: Slay the Spire relics that change what rooms hold (Tiny Chest, Juzu Bracelet), and Risk of Rain's artifacts that rewrite the run's rules. Different here: the key changes the arena's toys inside the fight, and those toys answer only to his elements. It is loot that changes the level, not the character.

3. Grave Heresies

The strongest heresies cost a move from his kit, the way the dark took his arm.

Departure Boons

How it works in play

  • From floor 2, a Boon offer may hold one grave heresy (at most one per offer, never in the first). It is two boons on one card, from one sign: a rule bend plus a cell, or a cell plus a number boon at rank II.
  • Its price is one move he holds, named on the card, closed for the rest of the run. The card shows the input and what it does afterwards, using the combo guide's own read (void_route()).
  • Never priced: the light string, the dash, the grab, Backlash and the held spirit's ender. A price is always a move with a way round it.
  • The look: the move list strikes the move through in crimson. Nothing on the model changes. In the fiction, the arm kept it.

The Bound Do Not Rise

Of the Bound. Grave. Payload C2 + C0

Heresy: Snap holds +0.5 s, and ice on a bound foe freezes it where it is held (Frozen in the Dark, frozen_dark).

Price: Singularity (H H). The second H does what it does with no hole to make.

The Bell Has No End

Of the Bell. Grave. Payload C2 + C1

Heresy: Aftermark (every breaker landing leaves its element's state) and Slow Bell at rank II.

Price: Tow Line. No pull-in after a dash: he has to walk to the crowd.

Worked example

  1. F1Ice spirit, Shard Cut, Cold Step. He opens fights with Overwhelm to bind, then freezes.
  2. F2 room 1The offer holds "The Bound Do Not Rise", priced at Singularity. He has used Singularity twice all run.
  3. He takes it.H H is gone; every Overwhelm now sets up a long, frozen Snap.
  4. F3He fights narrow and deep: bind, freeze, cut. A player who loves Singularity never takes this card, and plays a different run.
  • Replaces: nothing. A rarer card in the Boon offer.
  • Reuses: the gate the spirits already use (KitSlots.open() asks Spirits.allows(); a priced move is one more "closed" set), the fallback routing in void_route(), the guide showing only what a press will do, the move list.
  • Cost: the price gate and the card: C2, once. Each grave card's payload is boons that are already C0 to C2. Plus checks: every priced move must be tested closed (Simple kits' "gates can quietly break routes").
  • Risk: fewer moves means less variety, and style rank pays for variety. That tension is the point, but it may feel bad. Early signs: nobody takes one (cut it), or everyone prices the same move (then that move is dead weight in the base kit, which is worth knowing).
  • Nearest: The Binding of Isaac's devil deals (pay max health for an item); Hades' Chaos boons (a curse for a few rooms, then a blessing). Different here: the price is a press from your own kit, permanent, and you choose it knowing how you play. The run narrows into a style rather than getting weaker.

4. The Arm Eats

A relic he does not want, the arm eats, and keeps a taste of it.

Small twist Gear: the Graft slot

How it works in play

  • The floor card gets a third answer. E swaps, as now. Hold E (0.6 s) and the arm eats the piece on the floor.
  • An eaten piece is gone. Its first line stays, at half value, for the run: a taste. Long Reach eaten is +7% reach on Reap, Black Tide and Overwhelm, whatever Graft he wears.
  • At most 3 tastes. Each is a ring on the arm's deep purple cuff, the place the rarity band already sits. A Profane eaten gives its affix, never its rite.
  • Tastes add with worn lines under the stacking rules (add, never multiply, every number capped).
  1. Room 3He wears Hooked Barb (Long Reach). A Keen Leaf drops; he has no spear moves, so a taste of it is worthless. He leaves it.
  2. Room 5Censer Chain drops (Many Tips, Wide Crescent). Better than Hooked Barb. He swaps, then eats the Hooked Barb he just dropped: a taste of Long Reach.
  3. Room 9The arm reaches a bit further than the Graft says, and the cuff has one ring.
  • Replaces: "walk away". Today a piece you leave is simply gone.
  • Reuses: the Graft lines and their constants, the stacking table, the cuff band.
  • Cost: tastes C1 (the same constants at half). The hold on the floor card C1. The ring on the cuff C2 (a texture band, like the rarity band).
  • Risk: it is salvage, which Gear and boons left out on purpose. And if eating is always right, every drop becomes a compare plus a second compare. Keep the cap low.
  • Nearest: Isaac's Void (absorbs active items for their stats); salvage in Diablo. Different here: no menu and no material. A few seconds on the floor, one line kept, and you can see it on the arm.

5. Writ of Anathema

The church learns what he is and builds against it, and what it builds to hold his power is what his power uses.

Departure The run

How it works in play

  • The run counts which state he pays most (every payoff already goes through improvise() and improv_cell(); count by state).
  • At each floor's end a writ is read, one line over the warden's door: "Anathema: the one who freezes."
  • Rooms on the next floor lean toward the pieces built against that power (pools and grates for ice, grates and torches for storm, barrels and braziers for magma, pillars and hooks for the dark). In the world doc's terms every piece is half a conductor, so the church's answer is more toys for him. This is the world doc's open question 5 answered by play, not by a menu.
  • The next warden is warded against that state. The first 2 times it would take it, it does not: a seal flashes and the state slides off. He has to open on it with something else.
  1. Floor 1Ice spirit. About 60% of his payoffs are Shatter.
  2. Writ"Anathema: the one who freezes."
  3. Floor 2 roomsThe flooded cistern comes up twice. Linked pools, ice_rink: his best rooms.
  4. Floor 2 TurnkeyHarpoon slides off it twice. He binds it with Overwhelm, Snaps it, then the ward is spent and Frost Spear lands.
  • Replaces: random room weighting in the run, and gives the Turnkey a build-aware answer (run slice 7's "change course if the Turnkey dies to the same string").
  • Reuses: ROOM_DEFS as the room list, the state tells (comic_state_tell.gd), the parry flash or a breaker seal as the ward's look, Game's style rank (the ward pushes variety, which it already pays for).
  • Cost: the counter and room weighting C1. The writ line C1. The ward C3: a new visible behaviour on one foe, with an existing flash, and its word.
  • Risk: a ward reads as punishment. Keep it to wardens, two charges, and never on the state his only spirit can make with nothing else in the kit (the dark can always bind). Second: if a run has only one way to set a state early, the counter always picks it, and the writ says nothing new.
  • Nearest: Shadow of Mordor's Nemesis (foes remember you); Hades' Heat (chosen difficulty). Different here: the reaction comes from your play, not a menu, and half of it helps you.

6. Confession

Tithe becomes indulgences, paid by how well he fought, and the Rest room becomes a confessional.

Mostly reskin The run: currency and Rest

How it works in play

  • Indulgences are the run's tithe, renamed and paid differently: at a room's end, by the best style rank he reached (Game.RANKS): D 0, C and B 1, A 2, S and up 3. Not dropped by elites, not paid for Gear left behind.
  • The confessional (the Rest room's stall) sells three things instead of three items:
    • Indulgence: a Boon offer, 1 of 3.
    • Recant: give up one heresy he holds and take 1 of 3 from any article in its place. The only respec in a run.
    • Penance: swear a vow for the next floor ("no Backlash", "no string twice in a row"). Kept to the warden, it pays a Profane Graft.
  • The heal stays the Rest room's other choice, as now.
  1. F1He clears at S, S, A, B: 9 indulgences.
  2. RestRecant Long Thaw (never felt it); take Wildfire.
  3. Penance"No Backlash" for floor 2. He learns to dash out instead of answer, and the Turnkey pays a Profane.
  • Replaces: tithe's sources and the stall's stock (run slice 9).
  • Reuses: Game.rank() and RANKS, the menu_screen.gd pattern of the bond prompt, the Boon offer.
  • Cost: the currency C1, the confessional screen C2, each vow C2 (it has to be tracked and shown).
  • Risk: paying by rank gives more to players already doing well. Recant breaks Gear and boons' "never swap a boon"; it is offered here because a paid, rare recant is not a reroll button. If Zak wants no respec, drop Recant and keep the rest.
  • Nearest: Devil May Cry's orb bonus by style rank; Hades' Charon shop and bounties; Gungeon's challenge shrines. Different here: little. The rank-to-currency link is DMC's. The spin is the fiction and the recant.

Keep conventional

  • The offer shape. Pick 1 of 3, no reroll, signs, "only what you can use", "lean in". It is fast and players already know it. Renaming it is enough.
  • The Graft. Gear and boons' Graft (lines that name a move or a form, Profane rites) is already "the arm is the character". It needs no new spin; it gets the Shackle's move lines.
  • Rarities and fixed-value lines. Iron, Hallowed, Profane already carry the church's voice. No item levels, no ranges.
  • The floor card and the stacking rules. Plumbing. Add, never multiply; every number capped.
  • States, payoffs, words. The fight's language stays the fight's. No heresy gets a new comic word until it has earned one in play.
  • The spirit. It is already the unique piece. Nothing here touches it, except that keys are filtered by the element it carries.

This lane's pick

Keys of the Prison, with the Catechism skin

Keys give Gear a job Boons cannot do: the Graft changes moves, the Shackle changes rooms, Boons change rules. Three layers, three different things, which the hub's decision 11 worries about. Most keys are C1 data on systems already built, and the world doc already wrote them. The Catechism costs nothing and makes the Boon screen sound like this game.

Next, if Boons feel flat: Grave Heresies

The most "only in this game" idea here, and cheap because the spirit gate already closes moves. It is also the one most likely to feel bad. Try one card in slice 4's debug offer before building more.

Writ of Anathema is good but late (it needs real floors and the Turnkey). The Arm Eats and Confession are small and can wait for slices 5 and 9.

Decisions for Zak

  1. Is the church's prison the world (the world doc's open question 1)? Yes. Every idea on this page leans on it.
  2. Does the Shackle slot become the prison's keys, with its dash and counter lines moved to the Graft? Yes, built in run slice 5 right after the Graft, starting with Lamplighter's Hook, Bonding Iron and Sluice Key.
  3. Are Boons presented as heresies torn from the catechism? Yes. Names and the card screen only; settle it before the card screen gets art.
  4. Can a heresy cost a move from his kit? Try one grave card in slice 4's debug offer. Keep it only if you take it willingly in play.
  5. Does the church react to the build (rooms weighted by the writ, warden warded against his top state)? Rooms yes, from slice 6. The ward waits for the Turnkey in slice 7.
